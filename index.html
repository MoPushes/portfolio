I'm Mohammed. I live in Berlin.

What I like most is opening up a system and seeing how it really works. I start with the infrastructure. Where does the data sit, who can touch it, what talks to what. Once I know that, I know where I am. And then fixing things is easy.

Right now I'm finishing my B.Sc. in International Media and Computing at HTW Berlin. Alongside my studies, I work in first-level IT support at NOGGO, a gynaecological oncology research network.

The last thing I built was a thesis registration system. Five of us made it in a project management module. I worked across the stack with Vue 3 and PocketBase. PocketBase got us running fast, but it also showed me where a setup like that would stop scaling.

Away from the screen: I like to play chess, basketball, an go on hiking trips.
