I build web applications, backend to frontend.

I'm Mohammed. I live in Berlin.

What I like most is opening up a system and seeing how it really works. I start with the infrastructure. Where does the data sit, who can touch it, what talks to what. Once I know that, I know where I am. And then fixing things is easy.

Right now I'm finishing my B.Sc. in International Media and Computing at HTW Berlin. I build with Vue and Spring Boot. Part of every week I'm in first-level IT support at NOGGO, a gynaecological oncology research network.

The last thing I built was a thesis registration system. Five of us built it in a project management module. I worked across the stack, Vue 3 and PocketBase, and handled the PDF and Excel export side of it.

Away from the screen: chess, basketball, hiking, a camera I use more than I thought I would, and travel when I can afford it.

I'm looking for a junior developer role.
