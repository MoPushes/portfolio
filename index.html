I build web applications, backend to frontend.

I'm Mohammed. I live in Berlin and I write software.

The part I enjoy most is getting inside a system and finding out how it actually works, not how the documentation says it works. Once I understand it I want to make it faster, or smaller, or clearer to the next person who opens the file. That's most of why I do this.

Right now I'm finishing my B.Sc. in International Media and Computing at HTW Berlin. I build with Vue and Spring Boot, and I spend part of every week in first-level IT support at NOGGO, a gynaecological oncology research network.

The best thing I've worked on is a thesis registration system I built with four other people for HTW's design faculty. It's live and it's used every semester.

When I'm not at a screen: chess, basketball, hiking, a camera I use more than I expected to, and travel whenever I can afford it.

I'm looking for a junior developer role.
